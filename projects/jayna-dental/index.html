Heritage of Excellence

Serving Delhi for Over a Century — Since 1910

Founded by visionary Dr. C.R. Jayna in historic Old Delhi, Jayna Dental World has preserved uncompromised ethics, medical prestige, and patient dedication through 4 generations.

1910

Pioneering Roots

Dr. C.R. Jayna establishes one of Delhi’s earliest registered dental practices in Chandni Chowk, introducing modern dental ergonomics to pre-independence India.

1950s

Connaught Place Flagship

The clinic expands to M-75 Outer Circle, Connaught Place, becoming the landmark dental destination for diplomats, prominent leaders, and generational families.

1990s

Multi-Specialty Mastery

Incorporating board-certified specialists in Orthodontics, Prosthodontics, Periodontics, and Oral Surgery under one coordinated institutional roof.

2026

Digital 3D Dentistry

State-of-the-art intraoral digital scanners, AI smile analysis, CAD/CAM ceramic milling, and painless micro-endodontics with European sterilization standards.
